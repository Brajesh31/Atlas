# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers are expected to be respectful, inclusive, and constructive. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, and deliberately unsafe contributions are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainer, Brajesh Kumar, through the contact method in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially where possible.
